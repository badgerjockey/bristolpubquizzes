How the confidence score works

Each score is out of 100 and is my judgement against a fixed rubric.

  • Who says so. The pub's own page or post, or the quiz host's own schedule, starts at 60. A current local listing starts at 45. An undated aggregator starts at 20.
  • How recent. Up to 25 more when the newest evidence is from the last month, tapering to nothing after a year.
  • Agreement. Up to 10 more when independent sources give the same night.
  • Doubt. 15 to 25 off when sources disagree on the night, the pub has changed hands, or I could confirm the quiz but not its day.

Newest evidence is the date of the most recent thing that supports the entry. 18 Nov 2026 means a live page, listing or host calendar read on the day this was compiled. Undated means only aggregator listings with no date.

Limits of this list

  • It cannot be complete. Pubs that only advertise on a chalkboard, or in Facebook posts that search engines have not indexed, will be missing.
  • Facebook and Instagram were read through search-engine previews, not by logging in, so some post dates are the preview's date.
  • Quizzes pause over Christmas and bank holidays. Low and Medium entries deserve a phone call first.
  • Venues marked S Glos fringe sit just outside the city boundary in South Gloucestershire.
